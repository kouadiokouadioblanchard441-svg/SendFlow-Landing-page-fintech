---
name: SendFlow CTA placement
description: The homepage keeps signup and app-download actions in separate CTA positions.
---

Keep the large hero CTA connected to the configured registration label and URL. The lower CTA in the download section should use the download label and navigate to the localized download page (`/download` or `/en/download`).

**Why:** The user confirmed this separation after correcting a change that had replaced the hero signup button instead of the lower CTA.

**How to apply:** When changing homepage call-to-action buttons, preserve the hero signup action and modify only the lower download CTA unless the user explicitly asks otherwise.
